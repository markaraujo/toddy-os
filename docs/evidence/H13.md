## Critical qualification review

The complete original compiler/libc/binutils outcomes remain in H07 and their
source-backed ADRs. GCC's 72 FAIL/two XPASS and Binutils gp-gmon are classified,
not relabelled passes. Glibc's original 6779 PASS, 16 XFAIL, 583 UNSUPPORTED and
single io/tst-lchmod failure remain preserved. Required native-kernel follow-up
then passed **50/50 together**, including io/tst-lchmod and Systemd namespace
coverage, with unchanged binaries and exact final-image core hash binding.
Other unsupported cases were not silently promoted; the printf oracle group
was not rerun. See [native report](H12-native-regressions.json) and ADR-0102.

Berkeley DB follows the owner's explicit coverage change in ADR-0092: native
CuTest, production commit/abort/reopen/crash recovery and actual APT cache
interruption/corruption/rebuild passed. Full Tcl runs remain failed/cancelled.
APT's cache-error diagnostics can accompany exit zero; generation must reject
those diagnostics and verify index bytes before use, as the focused test does.

English/Portuguese cases passed. Three Greek default-matcher cases remain known
failures (ADR-0037). Expat retains unresolved issues; actual consumer enumeration
and final-live DBus input bounds passed, without a blanket vulnerability-fix or
universal sandbox claim. Systemd live shutdown reports busy read-only lower/media
unmounts before poweroff zero; no writable persistent disk was attached. These
qualifications are also in the owner guide. No unclassified critical acceptance
failure remains, and none of these qualifications accepts Phase 2 functionality.
