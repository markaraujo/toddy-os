## Reproduction and recovery

Use the frozen inputs and [BUILD.md](../BUILD.md), [RECOVERY.md](../RECOVERY.md),
and [LIVE.md](../LIVE.md). Production drivers are h11-python-qa-driver.sh and
h11-release-driver.sh; the latter composes the accepted release root, Squashfs,
initramfs and hybrid media. Both refuse unsafe/ambiguous existing generations.
The two failed integration generations and logs remain under the documented
h11-qa-notices-20261107 and h11-magic-marker-20261107 recovery directories.
Their original failures remain failures; neither required a package rebuild.

Artifact revision after H12 USB discovery: ADR-0101 changes only early-media
discovery. New initramfs/hybrid media passed composition, full payload readback
and independent artifact review. Root/compression and all 134 component records
remain unchanged. Original media and failing USB logs remain preserved in
[H12-first-media.json](H12-first-media.json). Complete revised boot matrix is
still mandatory; successful artifact assembly is not USB acceptance.
