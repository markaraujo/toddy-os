# Community conduct

Toddy OS welcomes people who want to improve the project, regardless of their
background or experience. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with
concrete evidence, and help newcomers find the relevant context.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private data,
post credentials or spam discussions. Critique the work rather than the person.
Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project and use sanitized examples.

The maintainer, [@markaraujo](https://github.com/markaraujo), moderates this
repository and may edit/remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads
or restrict participation. If a problem involves private information or a safety
concern, contact the maintainer through an established private channel rather
than repeating the material publicly. Security vulnerabilities and credential
exposure follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This is a small independent project. Participation does not create a guarantee
of an immediate response or acceptance of a proposed contribution.
