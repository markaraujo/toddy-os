01 — KEEP IT FAMILIAR

Just enough desktop.

GNOME and everyday essentials. OpenCode and VS Code as optional installs. Your computer starts useful, without a whole kennel of apps.

02 — MAKE IT YOURS

Your kind of clever.

Local AI or your provider. Editable profiles, persona and skills. A sensible Toddy default, with room to teach this dog your own tricks.

03 — STAY IN CONTROL

A paw on the brakes.

Visible tasks, revocable permissions and Stop. System administration under your policy, with a separate executor and a recovery path.
