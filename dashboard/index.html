Just enough desktop.

GNOME and everyday essentials. OpenCode and VS Code as optional installs. Your computer starts useful, without a whole kennel of apps.

02 — MAKE IT YOURS

Your kind of clever.

Local AI or your provider. Editable profiles, persona and skills. A sensible Toddy default, with room to teach this dog your own tricks.

03 — STAY IN CONTROL

A paw on the brakes.

Visible tasks, revocable permissions and Stop. System administration under your policy, with a separate executor and a recovery path.

What is actually ready?

H01-H13 complete; G1 passed in QEMU/KVM and foundation prerelease v0.1.0-foundation.1 is public. Phase 2 has been replanned for a minimal GNOME desktop and customizable native Toddy assistant, with optional local/API AI, developer apps and third-party driver setup. Navy brand assets and labeled UI/installer concepts are prepared; focused basic tests and small traceable deliveries replace exhaustive preview validation. B01-B13 guest implementation remains unstarted; G2 and physical hardware validation are pending.

H01 Inspect host and select isolated build environment completed · 4 points

Prerequisites: None

Technical acceptance criteria (English)

  • Inventory actual CPU, RAM, storage, virtualization and installed build tools before making changes; then install/configure the necessary host/guest dependencies under ADR-0003 and record what changed.
  • Choose a disposable VM and record resource budgets, target disk identity, host/guest boundaries and recovery plan.
  • Run the selected versioned book host-requirement check in the designated environment and record missing prerequisites; H02 performs the final source freeze.

B05 Persist tasks and recover interrupted system changes not started · 10 points

Prerequisites: B04

Technical acceptance criteria (English)

  • Tasks survive a service restart without duplicating a known side effect; uncertain outcomes wait for review and retries remain bounded.
  • Stop prevents queued/new actions and terminates or reports active managed processes; revoke immediately blocks new privileged dispatch.
  • One configuration rollback restores service operation, preserves a manual rescue path and reports drift without fighting user intent.
  • Invalid profile configuration preserves the working revision; one rollback and task-revision pinning check pass without unexpected replay.
Open story
B06 Deliver Toddy quick entry, native UI and first-run setup not started · 10 points

  • Basic keyboard/focus, EN/PT copy and Stop smoke pass; publish an honestly labeled interim text preview rather than claiming G2.
  • The user can customize the harness through profiles/settings, reject invalid changes, restore a revision and export/import without credentials or administrative grants.
